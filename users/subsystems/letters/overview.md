# Letters & Emailing — User Guide

Letters are template-based notices you can attach to a CE case, a property, or a permit
file. Each letter can optionally detail one or more code violations, and every letter goes
through the same 4-step flow whether you're creating it fresh or basing it on an earlier one.

Letters let you:

- **Generate** a notice from a reusable template, with the recipient, dates, and (for CE case
  letters) the case's violations filled in automatically — see
  [Generating and sending a letter](/users/subsystems/letters/generating-and-sending-a-letter).
- **Print** a finalized letter as a PDF, laid out per the template's print style — see
  [Printing a letter](/users/subsystems/letters/printing-a-letter).
- **Email** a letter directly from CodeNforce — not just record that it went out, but actually
  send it, with delivery status tracked automatically — see
  [Distributing a letter](/users/subsystems/letters/distributing-a-letter).
- **Distribute** it by any other channel — mail, posting/placard, door-hanger — and keep a
  single running history of every attempt, also covered in
  [Distributing a letter](/users/subsystems/letters/distributing-a-letter).

As of the August 2026 upgrade, the mailing-history panel you may remember on a letter is now
called **Correspondence** — it tracks every way a letter has gone out (mail, email, posting/
placard, door-hanger), not just mailing.

## Where letters live

You'll find the Letters / Correspondence panel — the same reusable component everywhere — in
three places:

- **CE Cases** — on the case's own page, alongside its violations and inspections.
- **Properties** — on the property's page, for correspondence not tied to a specific case.
- **Permit files (OccPeriods)** — on the permit file's page.

Because it's the exact same component in all three spots, once you know how to use it on a
case, you already know how to use it on a property or a permit file. Look for the help icon
(**?**) in the panel header to jump straight back to this guide from within the app.
