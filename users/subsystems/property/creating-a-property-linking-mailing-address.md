# Creating a Property and Linking a Mailing Address

1. Start on the dashboard.
2. Click **property search & groups**.
3. Click **Add new property**.
4. Enter the county parcel ID.
5. Click **Create property and go to profile**.
6. You'll see the parcel ID, but no mailing address yet.
7. Scroll down to **Mailing Address Links**.
8. Click **Link New Mailing Address**.
9. Search by zip code first.
10. Click **load streets**.
11. If the street does not already exist, click **New street in [zip code]**.
12. Enter the street name and click **Save Changes**.

    > **Tip:** After adding the street name, you'll have to search for it again.
13. Click the search field.
14. Type the street name.
15. Click **load mailing addresses**.
16. Click **New mailing address on [municipality]**.
17. Type the building number.
18. Click **Save changes**.

    > **Tip:** You'll have to search for the building number you just created.
19. Click **select for linking**.
20. Click **Link Parcel ID: [parcel id]**.
21. Click **Choose an association type**.
22. Click **property address**.
23. Click **Save changes**.
24. You now have a new property with its county parcel ID and mailing address linked.

    > **Tip:** You won't need to go through this process for every property in your muni —
    > only occasionally, for new builds or if a property was missed in the scrape from the
    > county site.
