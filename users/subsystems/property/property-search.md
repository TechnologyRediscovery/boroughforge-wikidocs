# Property Search

## Method 1 — Search & Groups

1. Start on the dashboard.
2. Click **property search & groups**.
3. Click the dropdown menu to pick a search criteria.
4. Review the options for property search.
5. The most frequently used is building number alone — this example uses building number and
   street name, which can help refine a search.
6. Enter the building number and street name for the property you want to view.
7. Click **Search**.

   > **Tip:** Different search functions result in different amounts of search results. It's
   > up to you to find your preferred search criteria.
8. When the search results load on the right side of the screen, find the property you need
   and click **view property**.
9. You'll be brought to the property landing page.

## Method 2 — Dashboard quick search

1. Start on the dashboard and scroll down below the event calendar.
2. Click the text field to enter the property number.
3. Click the adjacent field to enter the street name — you can also search one at a time.
4. Click **Search**.
5. Search results appear to the left of the search boxes.
6. Click **view property**...
7. ...to be redirected to the property landing page.
8. Switching municipalities will show a different set of results. Enter the building number...
9. ...and click **Search**.
10. Review the results.
11. Click **reverse order** to reverse the sort order of the results.
