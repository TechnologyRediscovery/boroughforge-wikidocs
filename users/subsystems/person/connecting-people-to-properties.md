# Connecting People to Properties

1. Start on the property landing page.
2. Scroll down to the **Person Links** box and click **Manage Links**.
3. Click **Create new person**.
4. Enter the name of the person you're creating.
5. You can also enter their job title, and select whether they are a multi-person name entry
   or a business name.
6. Click **Save changes**.
7. Click **Connect person to current property [property name]**.
8. Click **Choose an association type**.
9. Select the association type that best fits the role the person has with the property.
10. Click **Create link**.

    > **Tip:** Refresh the page in your browser to see the person link at the top of the page
    > or listed in the person links box (depending on the association type).
    >
    > **Tip:** Make sure mailing addresses are updated for any association type that would not
    > get mail at the property (property managers, management companies, non-occupant owners,
    > etc).
