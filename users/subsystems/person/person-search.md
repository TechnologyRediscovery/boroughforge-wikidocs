# Person Search

1. Person search can be started from anywhere. Navigate to the **Person** tab on the top menu
   bar and click **Person search/add person**.
2. Open the dropdown menu to see the different search criteria — "name" works most of the time.
3. Enter the name of the person you are searching for.

   > **Tip:** Person records are available across all municipalities that use CodeNforce.
4. Click **Search**.
5. Click **View** to see the person's profile.
6. On the person's profile, you can see the person's connections to properties, their mailing
   address, and any contact information you have for them.
