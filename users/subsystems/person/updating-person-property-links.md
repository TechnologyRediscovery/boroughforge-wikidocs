# Updating Person-Property Links

## Change an existing person's role

1. Start on the property landing page.
2. Scroll down to the **Person Links** box.
3. Click **View/Edit link** for the person whose role you're changing.
4. Click **Edit**.
5. Change their role — for example, from "owner-occupant of" to "formerly owned".
6. Click **Save changes**.
7. Close the link pop-up.

## Add a new person link

8. Click **Manage Links**.
9. Search for the new person first.

   > **Tip:** Searching before adding a new person can help you find people flagged for PA
   > Act 90, and prevents duplicate people from being added to the system.
10. If the person you searched for has no other property links in the system, click
    **Create new person**.
11. Enter their name and click **Save changes**.
12. Click **Connect person to current property [property name]**.
13. Click **Choose an association type**.
14. Select their role.
15. Click **Create link**.
16. Click **Connect person to Mailing Address** if needed.

    > **Tip:** This process is common for deed transfers or change-of-occupancy permits (if
    > required by your municipality). It's important to keep these updated — if you need to
    > issue a citation, it must be in the owner's name for legal purposes.
