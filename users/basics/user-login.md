# User Login

1. Navigate to [https://www.google.com/](https://www.google.com/) or your preferred search engine.
2. Search for "CodeNForce".
3. Click the CodeNforce result (`codenforce.org`).
4. Click "codeNforce".
5. Click "Login To Mission Control".
6. Enter your username and password.

   > **Tip:** Save this information in your browser for easy access later. This is also your login for the mobile app.
7. Select the municipality you are working in.

   > **Tip:** COG Land is the test database. You can experiment and practice different functions there without risking any "real" data.
   >
   > **Tip:** You can bookmark the municipality landing page for easy access later. In Chrome, select the star next to the address bar and save it to your bookmarks bar.
