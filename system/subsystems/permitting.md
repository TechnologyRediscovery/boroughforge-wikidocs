# Permitting (`permitting`)

Covers permit issuance and management. Permit files live on the occupancy side of CNF (as
opposed to the code-enforcement/violation side), but permitting is significant enough to
stand on its own rather than being folded into `occupancy`.

- **Developers:** [dev/subsystems/permitting/](/dev/subsystems/permitting/overview)
- **Admins:** [admin/subsystems/permitting/](/admin/subsystems/permitting/overview)
- **Code officers & staff:** [users/subsystems/permitting/](/users/subsystems/permitting/overview)
- **Public:** not applicable
